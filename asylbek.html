Interactive project

Monthly budget calculator

Enter your monthly income and total expenses in US dollars to see the difference. This calculator does not send or save your entries.

How this page works

HTML organizes the content. CSS controls the colors, spacing, and layout. JavaScript handles the budget calculation and the light and dark mode button.

The calculator subtracts expenses from income. If expenses are higher, it shows the amount over budget.
